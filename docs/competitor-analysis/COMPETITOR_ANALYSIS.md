# Competitor Analysis Report — OOH & DOOH Advertising Ecosystem in India

## Executive Summary
Before architecting **DreamsAdverstings**, we conducted a structured benchmark across the seven reference Indian OOH, DOOH, and creative advertising platforms specified in the project charter. Every design pattern, discovery filter, and workflow below is synthesized into original components for DreamsAdverstings without copying third-party code, proprietary assets, or written copy.

| Reference Platform | Primary Focus Studied | Key Observed UX & Functional Patterns | How DreamsAdverstings Innovates & Integrates |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[Pioneer Publicity](https://pioneer-publicity.com/)** | Corporate profile, services, credibility, Pan-India portfolio | 65+ year legacy narrative ("Intelligence, Thoughtfulness, Transformational"), 8-city presence (including Pune), portfolio segmented by Large Format, Street Furniture, Ambient, Transit, and Naming Rights. | Structured multi-format catalog (16 service verticals), verified vendor credibility badges, and multi-city database schema anchored in Pune. |
| **[Signpost India](https://signpostindia.com/media/)** | Premium design, corporate storytelling, urban media, pDOOH | Publicly listed enterprise positioning ([Q4FY26 IR Deck](https://signpostindia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/SIL-IR-Presentation-Q4FY26-Signed.pdf)), 12,500+ transit/DOOH assets across 25+ cities, ~95% LED DOOH, Captura & RoadStar audited audience measurement. | High-contrast `#101820` + `#D9A441` dark-luxury visual hierarchy, DOOH slot-level inventory specifications, and verified proof-of-display audit workflows. |
| **[AdOnMo](https://adonmo.com/)** | Digital advertising inventory, AdServe programmatic engine, self-serve flow | 66,000+ ambient screens across 27+ cities ([Our Products](https://adonmo.com/our-products/)), Residences/Corporates/Gyms/Malls segmentation, 5-minute self-serve map discovery, transparent inline pricing, and contextual triggers. | Self-serve interactive map pin discovery across 18 Pune zones, instant shortlist comparison, and direct online Razorpay test checkout. |
| **[RoshanSpace](http://www.roshanspace.com/)** | Flagship media locations, inventory discovery, traffic science | Large-format marquee structures backed by **Vehicle Detection Tech** for real-time speed/traffic impression verification. | Exact GPS coordinates (`Lat Long`), illumination tags (`FL`, `NL`, `BL`, `LED`), dimensions, and facing-direction metadata on all 156 Pune seed assets. |
| **[Setu Advertising](https://www.setuadvertising.com/)** | Pune creative agency presentation and case studies | 40-year Pune creative legacy organized into 3 clear pillars: Strategy + Content, Strategy + Communication, and Strategy + Digital. | Dedicated Case Study templates, Creative & Brand Activation service verticals, and Pune-local market intelligence. |
| **[The Media Ant](https://www.themediaant.com/blog/media-planning-definition-types-importance/)** | Advertising discovery, 8-step media planning, AI planning, quotations | Transparent rate cards ([OOH Guide](https://www.themediaant.com/blog/outdoor-advertising-examples/)), 8-step media planning lifecycle, AI-assisted location/budget allocation, and itemized quotation generation. | Built-in **AI Media Planner** + automated backend quotation engine calculating Display, Installation, Printing, Commission, and 18% GST. |
| **[Wallop Advertising](https://www.wallop.in/)** | Creative outdoor campaigns, 3D anamorphic DOOH, Pune flagship screens | Curved 3D anamorphic screens, Mumbai Kohinoor 1 & 2, and Pune DOOH landmarks ("Opal One – FC Road & Opal Two – RTO Chowk") with PMC/MSRDC partnerships. | Cinematic twilight hero presentation, DOOH 10-sec slot modeling (e.g., Baner Balewadi Chowk Signate Corner LED), and interactive campaign portfolio. |
